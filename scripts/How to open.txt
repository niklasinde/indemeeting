Meeting Recorder - how to install

Needs macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later.

1. Drag "Meeting Recorder" onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open it from Applications. macOS says "Apple could not verify..." because the app
   is not from the App Store. Click Done (not Move to Trash).

3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to Meeting Recorder. Confirm with your password.
   (Right-click > Open no longer does this on macOS 15 and later.)

4. The Setup window opens. Allow the Microphone and System Audio Recording,
   then record the 5-second test.

5. Look for the waveform icon in the menu bar. That is the app; it has no Dock icon.

Summaries use Apple Intelligence: turn it on in System Settings > Apple Intelligence & Siri.
The Siri language and the Mac's language must match for it to work.

Everything (recording, transcription, summaries) stays on your Mac.
Updates: drag the new version over the old one. Your permissions are kept.
